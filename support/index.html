How can we help?

For questions, a bug report or a feature request, email jasimraza@hotmail.com.

Tell us what happened

Include the app build (Settings → Help and support → Diagnostics and logs), iOS version, what you tried and what you expected. For reminder or timer issues, include the exact local scheduled and observed times.

Screenshots and diagnostic reports are optional. Review them and hide personal task information before sharing. Never send passwords or signing credentials. A diagnostic report is not a task backup.

Lock Screen and widgets

In the app, open Settings → Help and support → Guides for placement and interaction instructions. Show on Lock Screen starts or refreshes the task display. iOS controls rendering and how long an activity can remain active; refreshing does not reset that lifetime.

Settings → Lock Screen → Live Activity renewal explains optional user-created renewal automations. Cone & Quill cannot create or inspect your personal automations. Existing shortcuts named Renew FocusTasks keep working after the app rename.

Reminders and Focus

Check notification and alarm permissions, sound and device Focus settings. Reminders use the start and repeat settings you save. Focus tasks share a session clock; finishing a session does not complete the selected tasks.

Siri and Calendar

Try app-qualified Siri phrases, such as “Add a task in Cone and Quill,” then provide the requested title. Recognition depends on your language and system settings. Calendar changes apply to explicitly linked events; your Calendar account controls its synchronization. Unlinking an event differs from deleting it.

Your support information

We use messages and voluntarily shared reports to respond and investigate your request. We delete resolved support content from active support records within 30 days, except when retention is legally required. See the privacy policy for details.
