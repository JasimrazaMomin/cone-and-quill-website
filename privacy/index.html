Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy covers Cone & Quill and these support pages. Contact the developer at jasimraza@hotmail.com with privacy questions or requests to delete information you have sent to support.

Your tasks stay under your control

The app stores tasks, notes, hierarchy, preferences, reminder settings and Focus session information on your device. It has no app account, developer-operated task server or app-provided CloudKit task synchronization. Device backup and restore depend on Apple and your device settings.

Widgets and Live Activities use an on-device shared snapshot. You choose whether to show tasks on the Lock Screen. Other people may see information you display or share in screenshots.

Optional Apple services

Notifications and optional AlarmKit alarms use system services. Calendar permission is requested when you choose scheduling features. Explicitly linked event titles, notes and times can be read and changed; Calendar providers may synchronize events according to your account settings.

Siri and Shortcuts can access requested task information and actions. Voice processing and system-service handling follow Apple's settings and privacy practices. The app does not control those services.

Local diagnostics

The app keeps a bounded technical history locally during normal use. Reports may include task/activity identifiers, counts, navigation, timestamps, process/build/system information and error types or codes. Task titles and notes are not intentionally included in diagnostic trace fields. Recording selects the time interval for an exported report.

Reports are not automatically uploaded to the developer. You decide whether to copy or share one and where it goes. Copy places it on the system pasteboard. Review the report before sharing it. Diagnostic history is rotated locally and is not a backup of your tasks.

Support and feedback

If you email support, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to reply, investigate the issue or consider your suggestion. Sending feedback is optional and does not affect app functionality. No diagnostic attachment is sent automatically.

Support content is kept while the request is being handled and deleted from active support records within 30 days after resolution, except where retention is legally required. You may request deletion by email. Email providers process delivery and storage under their own policies; their backup retention may differ.

Tracking and this website

The app includes no advertising SDK, tracking SDK or automatic developer analytics upload. These static support pages add no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or contact form. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security, including for visitors who are not signed in. Hosting and delivery information is handled under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This is separate from the app’s on-device task storage.

Your choices and deletion

You can change Calendar, notification, alarm and Siri permissions in system Settings, edit or delete tasks in the app, and choose whether to display or share information. Deleting a task does not retract information already shared or necessarily delete a separately linked Calendar event. Removing the app removes its local app data; do not assume a source checkpoint is a task backup. Copies held by system backup services or external Calendar providers follow their own controls.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if the app's practices change. Questions and privacy requests: jasimraza@hotmail.com.
